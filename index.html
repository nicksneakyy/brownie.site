The ultimate brownie recipe.

A brownie like no man has ever seen. Buckle up ladies and gentlemen and present yourselves with:

  • A sturdy bat to scare the Boogeyman off.
  • Some napkins in case of world apocalypse.
  • A sturdy amount of morning coffee.

After that's ready, prepare the kitchen with:

  1. 115g of unsalted, molten butter
  2. 2 large eggs
  3. 200g granulated sugar
  4. 1 tsp vanilla extract
  5. 335g unsweetened cocoa powder
  6. 65g flour
  7. 1/4 tsp salt
  8. 90g chocolate chips (optional)

This recipe was made with love and the help of: Mozilla MDN

Let's make THE BROWNIE!!

Grab your chef de cuisine hat and your mechanix gloves then follow the following VERY carefully:

  1. Enlist your oven for war: preheat at 175°C/350°F. Grease your baking pan of 8 inches or line it with parchment paper (like my mom usually does).
  2. Mix the liquid ingredients: In a medium bowl, beat the melted butter and sugar together until they beg for mercy. Insert the eggs one at a time and season your masterpiece with the vanilla extract.
  3. Breed the solidified ingredients: Put in the choco powder, pulverized wheat (flour) and salt from the Himalaya beaches. Fold gently with a spatula until it's done. Do NOT overmix or the brownie will become brown terracotta.
  4. Fold in extras: Fold in the chocolate chips if you're wielding them.
  5. Cool down: Let the brownie chill and cut it in slices or else you'll get blasted in the face with hot steam and you won't even be able to eat it! It stings!
